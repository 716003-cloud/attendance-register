Privacy Policy — Attendance Register

Last updated: 22 October 2026

“Attendance Register” (the “app”) is a tool for teachers of Boborahim Mashrab o'quv markazi to prepare printable attendance registers (Excel files) for their study groups.

Data you enter

You may type group details and students' names, phone numbers, parents' phone numbers and addresses so that they appear in the register you generate.

Where the data stays

All of this data is stored only on your device, inside the app. The app does not send it to the developer or to any server, does not use accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and does not share data with third parties. The developer cannot see your data.

Files you create

When you generate an Excel file or a backup file, the app creates it on your device and opens the Android share sheet. Where the file goes afterwards (for example Google Drive, Telegram or e-mail) is decided only by you and is governed by the app you choose.

Deleting your data

You can delete a group or clear its students inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes all data the app stored.

Children

The app is intended for teachers and staff, not for children. It stores information about students only when the teacher types it in.

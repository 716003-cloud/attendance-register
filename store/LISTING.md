**Full description (max 4000):**
Attendance Register helps teachers prepare printed attendance registers quickly — without retyping students into Excel
for every group.

• Create a tab for each study group and enter its students once.
• Choose level and group language, and give every group its own colour theme.
• Press one button to get ONE Excel file: a front page, a register sheet for every group, and a shared back page.
• Registers keep the layout of the printed form: 15 student rows and 24 lessons plus one additional lesson. Attendance
  cells are always left empty so you can fill them in by hand after printing.
• Pick pastel colours for the attendance cells, or keep them white for classic printing.
• Works offline. Your students' data is stored only on your phone — no account, no ads, no tracking.
• Backup and restore your groups with a simple backup file.
